FAQs

Clearbot Class 3 – common questions

Need a configuration-specific answer? Contact our technical team with your project details.

What is Clearbot Class 3 designed for?

It is designed for floating waste collection, hydrographic and draft survey applications, and waterway monitoring, with a focus on ports, harbours and larger waterbodies.

How long can Class 3 operate?

The published battery life is 8 hours. Actual operating time may vary with operating conditions, payload and configuration.

Does Class 3 support solar charging?

Yes. Solar charging is listed as a charging option in the published Class 3 specifications.

What is its waste collection capacity?

The product page lists floating waste collection of up to 200 kg per hour. Actual throughput depends on the type and density of waste and site conditions.

Can Class 3 perform bathymetric or draft surveys?

The published product information lists hydrographic and draft surveys. Sensor payload and final survey configuration should be confirmed for each project.
