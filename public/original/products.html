Frequently Asked Questions

Talk to our team about your waterway, operating conditions and vessel configuration.

Which Clearbot product is suitable for compact waterways?

Fetch Skimmer is described as a compact vessel for confined and shallow water areas, including ports and marinas. Site suitability should be assessed against local conditions.

Which products support floating waste collection?

Fetch Skimmer, Class 2 and Class 3 are presented for floating waste collection. Alligator Neo is designed for aquatic vegetation management.

Does every product support solar charging?

No. Solar charging is listed for Fetch Skimmer and Class 2. The supplied Class 3 and Alligator Neo datasheets do not specify solar charging.

Can I request a technical demonstration?

Yes. Use the Discuss Your Project button to share your location, waterbody and operating requirements with our team.
