FAQs

Clearbot Class 2 – common questions

Need a configuration-specific answer? Contact our technical team with your project details.

What is Clearbot Class 2 designed for?

Class 2 is designed for floating waste collection, hydrographic and draft surveys, water quality monitoring and waterway monitoring across rivers, lakes, ports and harbours.

How much floating waste can it collect?

The published product information lists a collection rate of up to 80 kg per hour and a payload capacity of up to 200 kg. Actual results vary with waste type, density and site conditions.

How long can Class 2 operate on a charge?

The published battery life is 6–8 hours per charge. Actual operating time depends on operating conditions, payload and configuration.

Does Class 2 support solar charging?

Yes. Solar charging and battery swapping are both listed as charging options for Clearbot Class 2.

What is the operating range and speed?

The published specifications list a 5 km range and an average speed of 3 knots.

Can Class 2 support oil spill cleaning?

The published project information includes an oil spill cleaning deployment with Hong Kong’s Drainage Services Department, reporting 50 litres of oil removed. Discuss the project scope and configuration with our team.

Can Class 2 perform hydrographic and draft surveys?

Yes. The product page lists hydrographic and draft surveys, bathymetric mapping and water quality monitoring. Sensor payload and final survey configuration should be confirmed for each project.

How can we arrange a technical demo?

Use the Request a Technical Demo or Book a Technical Demo button to share your site details and operating requirements with the Clearbot team.
