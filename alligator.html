FAQs

Clearbot Alligator Neo – common questions

Need a configuration-specific answer? Contact our technical team with your project details.

What is Clearbot Alligator Neo designed for?

Alligator Neo is designed to remove dense water hyacinth and aquatic vegetation from ports, lakes and other heavily infested waterbodies.

How long can Alligator Neo operate?

The published battery life is 4 hours. Actual operating time may vary with operating conditions, payload and configuration.

Does Alligator Neo support solar charging?

Yes. Solar charging is listed as a charging option in the published Alligator Neo specifications.

What is its waste collection capacity?

The product page lists a vegetation-clearing rate of around 90 square metres per hour. Actual performance depends on vegetation density and site conditions.

Can Alligator Neo perform bathymetric or draft surveys?

The published specifications list a range of 3 km and an average speed of 2 knots.
