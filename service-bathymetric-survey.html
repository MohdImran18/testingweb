UNDERWATER MAPPING

Depth and terrain information for better-informed waterway decisions.

Bathymetric surveys map the depth and shape of underwater terrain. Clearbot's published service description highlights sonar technology and remote operation for collecting real-time data in lakes, rivers and coastal areas.

The resulting information can support navigation planning, construction activities and environmental studies. Survey design, coverage and final data outputs should be agreed against the project brief and site conditions.
